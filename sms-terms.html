SMS terms & conditions

The text messaging program run by Worldwide Auto Leasing.

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Program description

When you opt in to the Worldwide Auto Leasing Alerts program, we may send you text messages about your lease quote, vehicle availability, document and appointment reminders, delivery scheduling, customer care follow-ups and, if you have opted in to them, monthly lease specials.

How you opt in

You opt in by checking the SMS consent box on a form on jason.joe-miz.com, by texting a keyword we publish to our number, or by giving verbal consent to a team member, which we confirm with a text. Consent is not a condition of purchase or of receiving a quote.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies by your activity with us. During an active quote or delivery you may receive several messages a week; otherwise messages are occasional.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Check your mobile plan for details.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message to cancel. You will receive one final confirmation text and no further messages unless you opt in again. You can also opt out by emailing info@worldwideautoleasing.com.

Help and support

Reply HELP to any message, email info@worldwideautoleasing.com or call (732) 534-4430.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Supported carriers include AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon, and most regional carriers in the United States.

Privacy

Your mobile number and opt-in data are used only for this program and are never shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing. See our Privacy Policy.

Changes

We may update these terms; the current version always lives at this address.
